# Editing the Step One Art website

You edit the site in **Pages CMS**, a simple editor in your web browser. You never need to touch code. Every time you click **Save**, the change is stored, and the website updates a few minutes later once publishing is connected.

## Log in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**.
2. Sign in with the GitHub account or email invitation you were given.
3. Choose the **Step One Art** project. On the left you'll see **Artwork** and **Site settings**.

## Add a new artwork

1. Click **Artwork**, then **Add an entry** (or **New**).
2. Fill in the essentials at the top of the form:
   - **Title**: e.g. *Hillside Church*
   - **Description**: one or two sentences about the piece
   - **Price (USD)**: numbers only, e.g. `450`, or leave empty to show "Price on request"
   - **Availability**: usually *Available*
   - **Main photo** and **Main photo description** (see next section)
3. When you create the entry, Pages CMS also shows a **filename** (web address) suggested from the title, e.g. `hillside-church.md` → `steponeart24.com/artwork/hillside-church/`. You can edit it once when creating. Don't change it later, or old links will stop working.
4. Fill in the other fields as you know them:
   - **Artist**: e.g. *John Hyder* or *Anonymous donor*
   - **Source**: *John's original* or *Donated artwork*
   - **Category**, **Medium**, **Dimensions** (e.g. *24 × 18 in*), **Framing**
   - **Longer description**: optional
   - **Date added** (defaults to today)
5. Tick **Feature on the homepage** if you want it in the homepage grid, which shows up to six featured pieces, newest first.
6. Leave **Published** ticked to put it on the site, or untick it to save a draft that won't appear anywhere on the website.
7. Click **Save**.

## Upload photos

- **Main photo**: click the field, then **Upload**, and choose the photo. Then fill in **Main photo description**, a plain sentence describing what's in the picture, e.g. *"Oil painting of a green field under a cloudy blue sky."* It helps people who use screen readers.
- **More photos** (optional): click **Add**, upload a photo, and describe it. Close-ups, the piece on a wall, and the back or signature are all helpful.

**Photo tips**
- Use **JPG, PNG or WebP**. The site makes smaller copies automatically, so upload the best version you have (under about 10 MB).
- **iPhone photos (HEIC) won't upload.** Uploading from Safari on the iPhone usually converts them automatically. If not, go to **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible**, or share or export the photo as JPEG first.
- Shoot straight on, in daylight, without flash. Fill the frame with the artwork.

## Change a price

Open **Artwork**, click the piece, change **Price (USD)**, and click **Save**.

## Mark a piece reserved or sold

Open the piece, set **Availability** to **Reserved** or **Sold**, and click **Save**.
- **Reserved**: the page says the piece is on hold, and the "buy" button disappears.
- **Sold**: the page says it has sold, and the piece moves out of the default "Available" gallery view. It can still be seen under **Sold**.

To take a piece off the website entirely, untick **Published** and save.

## Site settings

**Site settings** holds the homepage heading and text, the mission text, social links, and the **Contact email**.
- **Contact email** is where purchase and donation inquiries go. Until it's filled in, the site says inquiries aren't open online yet.
- **Demo mode** must be switched **off** at launch, once titles and prices are filled in. While it's on, a "preview site" notice shows and search engines are asked not to list the site.

## If something doesn't show up

Changes appear on the website a few minutes after saving, once publishing is connected. If a change still hasn't appeared after about 10 minutes, a required field may be missing or a photo may be in the wrong format. Contact ManicWebmaster, who can see the exact error.
